Suggested Readings

Climate Change

Haines A, Ebi K. The imperative for climate action to protect health. N Engl J Med. 2019;380:263 [An excellent review of the health effects of climate change].

Air Pollution

Beelen R, Raaschau-Nielsen O, Massimo S, et al. Effects of long-term exposure to air pollution on natural-cause mortality: an analysis of 22 European cohorts within the multicenter ESCAPE project. Lancet. 2014;383:785 [A large European study relating all-cause mortality and particulate inhalation].

Heavy Metal Poisoning

Hanna-Attisha M, LaChance J, Sadler RC, et al. Elevated blood lead levels in children associated with the Flint drinking water crisis: a spatial analysis of risk and public health response. Am J Public Health. 2016;106:283 [An article that discusses public health issues resulting from water pollution with lead in Flint, Michigan].

Hon KL, Fung CK, Leung AKC. Childhood lead poisoning: an overview. Hong Kong Med J. 2017;23:616 [A discussion of lead poisoning in childhood].

Rehman K, Fatima F, Waheed I, et al. Prevalence of exposure of heavy metals and their impact on health consequences. J Cell Biochem. 2018;119:157 [A comprehensive paper on heavy metal poisoning].

Effects of Alcohol and Tobacco

Cao Y, Willet WC, Rimm EB, et al. Light to moderate intake of alcohol, drinking patterns, and risk of cancer: results from two prospective US cohort studies. BMJ. 2015;351:h4238 [A discussion of alcohol usage and cancer at different dose levels].

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Outbreak of Lung Injury Associated with the Use of E-Cigarette, or Vaping, Products. https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/basic_information/e-cigarettes/severe-lung-disease.html [CDC report on severe lung injury associated with use of flavored e-cigarettes].

Maddatu J, Anderson-Baucum E, Evans-Molina C. Smoking and the risk of type 2 diabetes. Trans Res. 2017;184:101 [A discussion of accumulating evidence for association of type 2 diabetes and tobacco smoking].

Rigotti NA. Balancing the benefits and harms of e-cigarettes: a national academies of science, engineering, and medicine report. Ann Intern Med. 2018;168:666 [A summation of the evidence linking e-cigarette use and disease].

US Department of Health and Human Services. The health consequences of smoking—50 years of progress: a report of the surgeon general. Atlanta, GA: US Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Chronic Disease Prevention and Health Promotion, Office on Smoking and Health; 2014 [A comprehensive report by the US Surgeon General on the health effects of smoking].

Menopausal Hormone Therapy and Oral Contraceptives

Gialeraki A, Valsami S, Pittaras T, et al. Oral contraceptives and HRT risk of thrombosis. Clin Appl Thromb Hemost. 2018;24:217 [A succinct review of the effects of hormone replacement therapy and oral contraceptives on thromboembolism].

Drugs of Abuse and Radiation Injury

Guy Jr GP, Zhang K, Schieber LZ, et al. County-level opioid prescribing in the United States, 2015 and 2017. JAMA Internal Medicine published online February 11 2019 E1 [A short article on the magnitude of the US opioid crisis].

Matthew JD, Forsythe AV, Brady Z, et al. Cancer risk in 680,000 people exposed to computed tomography scans in childhood or adolescence: data linkage study of 11 million Australians. BMJ. 2013;346:f2360 [A paper showing that children who have undergone CT scans have a 24% increased risk of cancer, adding to accruing evidence that CT scans increase the risk of secondary cancers in children and adolescents].

National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA). https://www.drugabuse.gov updated in June 2019; [An extensive discussion of the effects of marijuana use and abuse including possible addiction].

Malnutrition

Hariz A, Hamdi MS, Boukhris I, et al. Gelatinous transformation of bone marrow in a patient with anorexia nervosa: an uncommon but reversible etiology. Am J Case Rep. 2018;19:1449 [A discussion of gelatinous transformation of bone marrow that is pathognomonic of anorexia nervosa].

Million M, Diallo A, Raoult R. Review. Gut microbiota and malnutrition. Microb Pathog. 2017;106:127 [An excellent discussion of the potential role of microbiome in pathogenesis of malnutrition].

Vitamins

Abdelhamid AS, Brown TJ, Brainard JS, et al. Omega-3 fatty acids for the primary and secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. (7):2018 CD003177; [A critical appraisal of the rate of omega-3 fatty acid supplementation and prevention of heart disease].

Padayatty SJ, Levine M. Vitamin C: the known and unknown and Goldilocks. Oral Dis. 2016;22:463 [A detailed discussion of vitamin C physiology and diseases associated with deficiency].

Rautiainen S, Manson J, Lichtenstein AH. Dietary supplements and disease prevention—a global review. Nat Rev Endocrinol. 2016;12:407 [A broad overview of the role of dietary supplements including vitamins in disease prevention].

Sarri JC. Vitamin A metabolism in rod and cone visual cycles. Annu Rev Nutr. 2012;32:125 [A discussion of the biochemical mechanism of vitamin A in vision].

Teng J, Pourmand A, Mazer-Amirshahi M. Vitamin C: the next step in sepsis management?. J Crit Care. 2018;43:230 [An appraisal of the possible role of vitamin C in sepsis management].

Obesity

Aune D, Sen A, Prasad M, et al. BMI and all-cause mortality: systematic review and non-linear dose-response meta-analysis of 230 cohort studies with 3.74million deaths among 30.3 million participants. BMJ. 2016;353:i2156 [A review of several large studies linking obesity with all-cause mortality].

Berthoud H-R, Munzberg H, Morrison CD. Blaming the brain for obesity: integration of hedonic and homeostatic mechanisms. Gastroenterology. 2017;152:1728 [A discussion of neurohumoral mechanisms and obesity].

Bouter KE, van Raalte DH, Groen AK, et al. Role of gut microbiome in the pathogenesis of obesity and obesity-related metabolic dysfunction. Gastroenterology. 2017;152:1671 [A balanced review of the role of the microbiome in obesity].

Chechi K, Nedergaard J, Richard D. Brown adipose tissue as an anti-obesity tissue in humans. Obes Rev. 2014;15:92 [A discussion of the role of brown adipose tissue in energy homeostasis].

Chen Y, Copeland WK, Vedanthan R, et al. Association between body mass index and cardiovascular disease mortality in east Asians and south Asians: pooled analysis of prospective data from the Asia Cohort Consortium. BMJ. 2013;347:f5446 [A discussion of the high risk of cardiovascular disease with obesity in a South Asian population].

Gonzalez-Muniesa P, Martinez-Gonzalez M-A, Hu FB, et al. Obesity. Nat Rev Dis Primers. 2017;3:1 [A general review of obesity with focus on visceral and truncal obesity].

Steele CB, Thomas CC, Henley SJ, et al. Vital signs: trends in incidence of cancers associated with overweight and obesity—United States, 2005-2014. MMWR Morb Mortal Wkly Rep. 2017;66(39):1052 [A discussion of the association of cancers with obesity].

Timper K, Bruning JC. Hypothalamic circuits regulating appetite and energy homeostasis: pathways to obesity. Dis Model Mech. 2017;10:679 [An excellent discussion of energy homeostasis including neurohumoral mechanisms].

Zhao VW, Scherer PE. Adiponectin, the past two decades. J Mol Cell Biol. 2016;8:93 [A review of the role of adiponectin in regulation of fat metabolism].
